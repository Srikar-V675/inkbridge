# 0075: Shapes flyout and properties card

Status: accepted (INK-175). Owner check pending: tablet touch (see Consequences).

## Context

Excalidraw's own properties panel is hidden (INK-117, decision 0066), and the rail had one Shapes button that cycled rectangle, ellipse, diamond and arrow on each tap. The owner could not pick a shape directly, could not reach line, and could not change colour, width, fill, font or layer order of anything drawn. Excalidraw 0.18.1 has no action API: it exposes `updateScene`, `getAppState`, `onChange` and `setActiveTool`, not its property actions.

## Decision

- **Pen and highlighter reset the shape defaults.** Freedraw copies `currentItemStrokeColor`, `BackgroundColor` and `FillStyle` when a stroke starts, so a blue background or a soft-ink stroke picked on a shape tool would leak into the pen. Choosing Pen or Highlighter on the rail now patches the background to transparent, the fill to solid and the stroke colour to the rail's last chosen pen colour (`railStore` remembers it from `setPen` and from any pen colour it sees in the defaults, so the rail swatch stays consistent). Browser-checked: a pen stroke and a highlighter stroke after a blue hachure rectangle with a soft-ink stroke are charcoal, transparent, solid.
- **Shapes flyout.** The Shapes button shows the last chosen shape; a second tap toggles a card of five buttons (rectangle, diamond, ellipse, arrow, line). The flyout is a `position: fixed` sibling of the rail `<nav>`, placed from the button's `getBoundingClientRect()`: INK-170 makes the rail scroll, and an overflow-hidden rail would clip a child. It closes on pick, on Escape, and on a pointer down elsewhere. Coming from a non-shape tool the pick resets opacity and width to solid (so a shape never inherits the highlighter); from another shape it leaves the width alone.
- **When the card shows** (`propsLogic.sourceFor`): a non-empty selection shows the selection card; otherwise an active rectangle, diamond, ellipse, arrow, line or text tool shows the tool card. Pen, highlighter, eraser, hand and laser never open it, so it never covers handwriting (the owner may want the pen on it later). A selection that holds an element with `customData.draft` hides it: a draft is the agent's, DraftBar owns it, and a restyle must not touch agent output (`applyStyle`, `applyFlag` and `unlockAll` skip drafts, deleted elements and, for styles, unsupported kinds). Rows are the controls every selected kind supports (`controlsFor`); a mixed value shows no pressed option.
- **Placement and look.** `Card` primitive, 280 px, left 92 px, top `var(--ink-rail-top, 88px)` (INK-170's variable, with the 88 px fallback), z-index 6, the chunk overlays' layer, but rendered after them in `Shell`, so the card is above chunk chips and their taps (review finding: at 4 the chips painted over it and stole taps while Chunks was on); under the top bar (7) and the Ask panel (8). The flyout is z-index 7 so it paints over the card (the top bar's controls sit above y 64 and the flyout starts lower). Browser-checked with Chunks on and a chunk chip under the card: the card is topmost there, and the flyout is topmost over the card. Header with Collapse (collapsed = header only, session state), sticky when a tall card scrolls; `max-height` keeps it inside the viewport and it scrolls (`overscroll-behavior: contain`). Tokens only; the stroke palette is pen-charcoal, pen-blue, pen-brick, soft-ink, dot (never the agent colour, never alarm); a test resolves them against `tokens.css`. No new token, no front matter change.
- **How edits are written.** A selection edit copies each changed element with `version + 1`, a new `versionNonce` and `updated` (`restyle`, the same three lines as Excalidraw's `newElementWith`), and passes the whole element list including deleted ones to `updateScene` with `CaptureUpdateAction.IMMEDIATELY`, so it is an undo step and the rail's Undo works. `sceneSync` sends any element whose version differs, so the edit is saved in the next delta. Tool-card edits write `appState.currentItem*` with `NEVER`. Selection edits never write the defaults, so recolouring a shape does not change the pen colour on the rail; tool-card edits do. While Excalidraw's text editor is open (`editingTextElement`) edits go to the defaults only: editing a live text box through `updateScene` is not verified.
- **Layers, duplicate, delete.** They depend on fractional indexes, groups, bound text and arrows, so they are not reimplemented: the card dispatches each action's own shortcut as a `keydown` on `.excalidraw` (ctrl or cmd with `[` and `]` for one step, ctrl+shift or cmd+alt for the ends, ctrl or cmd+D, Delete), the way the rail sends undo. Browser-checked: send to back, bring to front, backward, forward, duplicate and delete all reach the saved scene (indexes `Zz`, `a1` for the order).
- **Lock.** Excalidraw removes a locked element from the selection and offers no way back, so Lock is a `locked` patch that also clears `selectedElementIds` in the same `updateScene`, and "Unlock all" (in the card whenever the scene holds a locked element) is the one path that edits elements outside the selection.
- **Text size and font.** Excalidraw re-measures text with an internal function. We measure ourselves (`measureText.ts`: widest line by canvas `measureText`, height = lines x font size x the element's `lineHeight`) and grow the box about its centre like Excalidraw's own adjustment; the family is loaded with `document.fonts.load` first (Nunito and Cascadia load lazily). A family change also patches `lineHeight` to the new family's value (Excalifont 1.25, Nunito 1.35, Cascadia 1.2, read from Excalidraw's font metrics; a test pins them) for unbound text and fits the box with it, as Excalidraw's own font action does; a size change keeps the element's `lineHeight`. Browser-checked: width and height after a size or family change matched a re-measure for single-line text (the first browser pass did not exercise the line-height change; the multi-line fit is covered by unit tests only). Limits: bound text (`containerId`) and fixed-width text (`autoResize === false`) change only `fontSize` and `fontFamily` and keep their box; a selection of the container alone does not include its bound text, so the card does not restyle it (not browser-checked: the bound-text case is covered by a unit test only).
- **Arrow type and arrowheads.** Converting a drawn arrow to or from elbow needs Excalidraw's elbow router, so a selection offers sharp and round only and an elbow arrow has no row; the tool default (`currentItemArrowType`) offers all three, so a new arrow can be elbow (browser-checked). Excalidraw 0.18 gives only arrows arrowheads: a line has none, so lines get no arrowhead rows. Edges (sharp, round) apply to rectangle, diamond and line.
- **Hashes and the chunker.** The hash allow-lists are untouched. `stroke_hash` reads `id,type,x,y,width,height,angle,points,pressures,isDeleted` and `content_hash` reads `type,width,height,angle,points,pressures,text,fontSize,fontFamily` plus bbox-relative x and y; neither reads colour, width, opacity, fill, stroke style, roughness, roundness or `locked`. So a colour, width, opacity, fill, edge or lock change changes no hash, causes no recognition call and no `png_stale`, and the stored crop PNG keeps its old look until the ink itself changes; the reading is unaffected (known limit). A text `fontSize` or `fontFamily` change changes `content_hash`, and the new `width` and `height` change `stroke_hash`: the chunk is re-read (no model call for agent text) and its crop refreshed, as the spec expects for changed ink; the chunker reads `width`/`height`/`x`/`y`/`text`, so a resize can move a chunk boundary, a style change cannot. Freedraw `points`, `pressures` and `simulatePressure` are kept by construction (the patch builders never emit them, the copy spreads the element) and by test; browser-checked on a seeded pressure stroke.
- **Cost on every change.** The store hooks `api.onChange` and reads only `appState` first. While a gesture is in flight (`cursorButton` down, `newElement`, drag, resize, rotate) it returns without reading elements or notifying; otherwise it compares a small key (selection object identity, tool, editing flag, the `currentItem*` defaults) and, only when something changed (or, for a selection, the elements array changed), reads the selected elements, and scans for a locked element only while the card can show. A pen stroke costs O(1). Applying a colour to a selection of 1000 strokes sends 1000 changed elements in one delta save (acceptable).
- **Touch (INK-173).** A finger tap on an element with the selection tool selects it (Excalidraw sets the selection on pointer-up) and the card appears when the finger lifts, because the store skips the change while the pointer is down. The card is outside `.ink-shell__canvas`, so a tap on it is never routed to the canvas and does not deselect; a tap on empty canvas deselects and hides it. Browser-checked with touch emulation. Placement above the selection is not built: the card sits beside the rail.

## Consequences

- INK-170 (rail sizing) and INK-174 (perf: onChange, PaperDots) stay independent: no `shell.css`, `App.tsx` or rail sizing edits; the flyout is not a child of the rail, and the card uses `--ink-rail-top` with a fallback.
- Layers, duplicate and delete depend on Excalidraw 0.18's keyboard shortcuts: a package bump needs the browser check again.
- The pen's width and colour on the card are out (rail presets only); the pen and highlighter do not open it.
- Owner checks on the tablet: a finger tap selects a drawn shape and the card appears; every row is easy to hit and the card scrolls on a short screen; Collapse gets it out of the way while writing; the flyout is reachable with the rail scrolling; colour, width, fill and opacity look right on real pen strokes; text size and font keep the text where it was; whether the pen and highlighter should also open the card.
